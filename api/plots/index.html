    Plots¶

    sparc.plots ¶

    Plots of SPARC explanations.

    Every function takes an :class:sparc.Explanation (or a subset of one, explanation[i]), draws with matplotlib on the given or a new axes and returns the axes; show=True calls plt.show(). The plots follow the conventions that users of attribution libraries know, at the concept level:

    bar mean absolute attribution of every concept, or the signed attribution of one instance, with the support of the fuzzy number as a whisker; waterfall from the base value to the model output of one instance, concept by concept; beeswarm one row per concept, one dot per instance, coloured by the concept score of the instance; heatmap instances by concepts, instances ordered by the model output; dependence attribution of a concept against its concept score, coloured by the concept with the strongest interaction; force the contributions of one instance pushing the output away from the base value; decision the cumulative path of every instance from the base value to its output through the concepts.

    The views that only SPARC can draw:

    interval_bars point, core and support of every attribution with the sign confidence and the model class disagreement; linguistic_profile the degree of every linguistic label per concept; disagreement the attribution distribution of every model class; concept_graph the fuzzy partition as a picture of the features; front the Pareto front with the knee, the fidelity optimal member and the current explanation; front_navigator the explanation along the front, member by member; selection_membership how often the front retains every concept and feature; coverage coverage of a replicated attribution by the intervals.

    fuzzy_attribution, linguistic_bars, pareto_front and membership of :mod:sparc.plotting are re-exported here.

    bar ¶

    bar(explanation, max_display=10, instance=None, ax=None, show=False)

    Concept importance bars.

    Without instance (and with more than one explained instance) the bars are the mean absolute attribution of every concept over the explained instances, with the support of the fuzzy global importance as a whisker. For one instance the bars are the signed attributions with the support of the fuzzy attribution. Retained concepts are marked with an asterisk.

    waterfall ¶

    waterfall(explanation, instance=None, max_display=10, ax=None, show=False)

    From the base value to the model output of one instance, one step per concept, the smallest steps collapsed.

    beeswarm ¶

    beeswarm(explanation, max_display=10, ax=None, show=False, cmap='coolwarm')

    One row per concept, one dot per instance at its attribution, coloured by the instance's concept score.

    heatmap ¶

    heatmap(explanation, max_display=10, instance_order='output', ax=None, show=False, cmap='coolwarm')

    Attributions of every instance (columns) and concept (rows), with the model output above and the importance beside.

    dependence ¶

    dependence(explanation, concept, color=None, ax=None, show=False, cmap='coolwarm')

    Attribution of one concept against its concept score, coloured by the score of an interacting concept.

    concept and color are concept names or indices; color=None picks the concept whose score correlates most with the residual of the linear trend, color=False draws without colour.

    force ¶

    force(explanation, instance=None, max_display=8, ax=None, show=False)

    The contributions of one instance as segments pushing the output away from the base value.

    decision ¶

    decision(explanation, max_display=10, instances=None, ax=None, show=False, cmap='coolwarm')

    Cumulative path of every instance from the base value to its output through the concepts, ordered by importance.

    interval_bars ¶

    interval_bars(explanation, instance=None, max_display=10, ax=None, show=False, compact=False)

    Attribution of one instance with its core and support, the sign confidence and the model class disagreement.

    The bar is the point attribution, the thick line the core of the fuzzy number (central 50 percent of the pool by default), the thin line its support (central 90 percent). Where the model classes disagree beyond the threshold the attribution is of type 2 and the centroid of every model class is marked with a tick. The sign confidence and the disagreement index are written beside every bar.

    linguistic_profile ¶

    linguistic_profile(explanation, instance=None, max_display=10, ax=None, show=False, cmap='Blues')

    Degree of every linguistic label for every concept of one instance, the best label marked.

    disagreement ¶

    disagreement(explanation, instance=None, max_display=8, ax=None, show=False)

    Attribution distribution of every model class, concept by concept, with the disagreement index.

    For one instance (instance or a one instance explanation) the dots are the resamples of every model class; without an instance the dots are the mean absolute attributions over the explained instances per resample and model class. The model dependence of an attribution is what no feature level method reports.

    concept_graph ¶

    concept_graph(explanation, min_correlation=0.3, ax=None, show=False, seed=0)

    Features placed by the similarity of their correlation profiles, coloured by concept with membership as opacity.

    Edges join features whose absolute correlation (over the explained instances, or over the training sample statistics when available) is at least min_correlation; the node size follows the mean absolute feature level attribution. A fuzzy partition shows as mixed colours and as nodes between the groups.

    front ¶

    front(explanation, ax=None, show=False, annotate=True)

    The Pareto front of explanation configurations with the knee, the fidelity optimal member and the current one.

    Fidelity loss against complexity, coloured by instability; dominated configurations in grey. The knee is the default explanation, the fidelity optimal member the most faithful one, and the current configuration is marked when the explanation was built at another front member with explanation.at.

    front_navigator ¶

    front_navigator(explanation, instance=None, n_members=4, max_display=6, axes=None, show=False)

    Small multiples of the explanation along the front, from the simplest to the most faithful member.

    Each panel is the interval bar view of the chosen instance (or the global importance when instance is None and the explanation holds several instances) at one distinct front member, with the member's objectives in the title. The knee and the fidelity optimal member are always included.

    selection_membership ¶

    selection_membership(explanation, ax=None, show=False, features=True)

    How often the Pareto front retains every concept, and every feature, across all its members.

    The selection membership of a concept is the share of the distinct front explanations that retain it (Section 3.5); it measures the robustness of a concept to the choice of the configuration, which a single explanation cannot show. With features the feature level membership is drawn beside it.

    coverage ¶

    coverage(explanation, replicate, ax=None, show=False)

    Coverage of a replicated attribution by the support and the core of the fuzzy numbers, per concept.

    replicate is an independent attribution of the same instances, at the feature level (shape (n_explain, n_features), aggregated by membership) or at the concept level (shape (n_explain, K)), for example the attribution of a model refitted on other data. The nominal levels of the support and the core are drawn as dotted lines; bars above them mean the intervals are conservative, below them that they are too narrow.

    Earlier plot functions¶

    sparc.plotting ¶

    Plotting helpers (matplotlib is an optional dependency).

    The figures follow a small, fixed categorical palette assigned in order, thin marks, recessive axes and direct labels where useful.

    plot_fuzzy_attribution ¶

    plot_fuzzy_attribution(explanation, instance, retained_only=True, ax=None, scale_axis=False)

    Membership functions of the fuzzy attributions of one instance, one curve per concept.

    plot_linguistic_bars ¶

    plot_linguistic_bars(explanation, instance, retained_only=True, ax=None)

    Concept bars with the centroid, support as a whisker and the best label as text.

    plot_pareto_front ¶

    plot_pareto_front(table, ax=None, highlight_knee=True)

    Fidelity loss against complexity for evaluated configurations, colored by instability.

    plot_membership ¶

    plot_membership(partition, ax=None)

    Heat map of the feature by concept membership matrix.

    plot_reliability ¶

    plot_reliability(table, ax=None, label='sign confidence')

    Reliability diagram from the table of sign_confidence_calibration.

    plot_feature_bars ¶

    plot_feature_bars(values, feature_names, ax=None, top=15, title='feature level attribution')

    Plain feature level bar plot of an attribution vector.
